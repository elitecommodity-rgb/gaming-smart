Safeguards in the system

Access controlIndividual accounts with staff, supervisor and admin roles. ID numbers masked for staff.
PasswordsAt least 10 characters, stored hashed, lock-out after 5 failed sign-ins in 15 minutes, sign-out after 8 hours.
Audit trailClient record views, exports, removals, imports, voids and sign-ins are logged with user and time.
Notice at captureA client cannot be saved without confirming the privacy notice was given. Existing clients can be marked from their record.
Data subject rightsExport a person's data (supervisor), correct details, remove personal details (admin).
RetentionInactive clients past {{ years }} years can be anonymised below. ID photos and proof of address are deleted with them.
Transport and storageHTTPS, secure and HttpOnly cookies, no-store page caching, files only served to signed-in staff.
