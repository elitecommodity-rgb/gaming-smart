An Excel file can hold several sheets, for example Machines, Clients and Entries, and each is filed on its own. Repeated imports are safe: rows already in the app are skipped. Dates can be 2026-03-14 or 14/03/2026; amounts may carry an R, spaces or commas. PDFs must contain real text (not a scan or photo). For scanned paper use the photo capture instead. Imported entries are marked as imported history and have no slip photo, so the BI board counts them as not photo-backed.
